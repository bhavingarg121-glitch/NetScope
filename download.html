Step-by-Step Installation

How to Install NetScope on Your Android Device

STEP 1
Download the APK

Tap the download button above to save app-debug.apk to your device's Downloads folder.

STEP 2
Allow Sideloading

When prompted by Android, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.

STEP 3
Grant Permissions

Open NetScope and allow Telephony permissions so the app can read raw hardware signal metrics.

Privacy & Permissions

Why NetScope Requests Permissions

Phone / Telephony State (READ_PHONE_STATE) Required by Android OS to query active SIM cards, carrier name, and cellular technology (5G NR / LTE).
REQUIRED
Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) Required by Android security policy to access raw cell tower IDs (CID, TAC, PCI) and RF signal levels (dBm/RSRP).
REQUIRED
Internet & Network State (INTERNET) Used strictly for performing socket speed tests and latency ping measurements to test endpoints.
