How to Install NetScope on Android

Follow these simple steps to install the NetScope APK package on your phone.

01

Download APK

Click the download button above to save app-debug.apk to your device's Downloads folder.

02

Allow Unknown Apps

If prompted by Android, enable "Install Unknown Apps" for your browser (Chrome/Firefox/Files).

03

Tap Install

Open the downloaded APK from notification drawer or Files app and tap "Install".

04

Grant Telephony Access

Launch NetScope and grant Phone State / Location permissions to start reading cellular signal.
