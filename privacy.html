Privacy & Permissions

Privacy & Data Transparency

NetScope operates under a strict privacy-first principle: no third-party trackers, no accounts, and on-device processing.

1. Telephony & RF Data

NetScope reads hardware signal properties (dBm, RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, and cell tower IDs) through Android's TelephonyManager APIs. This data is computed locally on your device to display live diagnostics.

2. Location Permissions

Android OS requires location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) to access cellular tower identifiers. NetScope does not track your continuous physical movement or upload personal location logs.

3. No Advertisements or Third-Party SDKs

NetScope is an open-source utility without marketing trackers, analytics SDKs, or ad monetization frameworks.
