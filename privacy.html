Privacy Policy & Permissions

Complete transparency on how NetScope India interacts with your Android device hardware.

1. Our Privacy Commitment

NetScope India is built as an open utility for everyday users and network enthusiasts. We do not collect, sell, lease, or monetize your personal data. All network speed tests and cellular signal measurements remain processed locally on your smartphone.

2. Why Does NetScope Require Permissions?

📞 READ_PHONE_STATE

Purpose: Required by the Android OS TelephonyManager API to detect active SIM cards, operator carrier names (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL), network roaming state, and connection generation (5G NR, 4G LTE, 3G, 2G).
What we DO NOT do: We do NOT access phone call history, contacts, or make phone calls.

📍 ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

Purpose: Under Android's security architecture, cell tower information (Cell ID, TAC, RSRP, SINR, Signal dBm) is classified as location-derived metadata. Android requires apps to request location permission to read cell tower signal strength.
What we DO NOT do: NetScope does NOT log GPS coordinates or transmit your location to any remote servers.

🌐 INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Purpose: Used exclusively to run on-demand download/upload network speed tests, ping checks, and DNS resolution diagnostics when requested by the user.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

NetScope contains zero third-party advertising SDKs. There are no tracking pixels or cross-app identifier beacons embedded inside the application package.

4. Open Source & Auditability

The NetScope project is open source and accessible on GitHub at github.com/bhavingarg121-glitch/NetScope. Anyone is welcome to inspect the source code to verify our privacy standards.
