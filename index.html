🛠️ Mobile Network Problem Troubleshooter

Select what you are currently experiencing to view root causes and technical recommendations.

🐌 Slow Internet Speed Diagnosis

Common Root Causes:
  • Tower Bandwidth Congestion: High number of users connected to the same cell sector.
  • Low SINR (Noise Ratio): Signal is experiencing heavy cross-talk or physical building interference.
  • Fallback to Saturated 4G: Phone dropped off 5G NR band due to indoor wall attenuation.
⚡ NETSCOPE RECOMMENDATION:

Check your real-time RSRP and SINR with NetScope Android. Moving closer to a window or toggling Airplane Mode can reconnect you to a cleaner frequency band.
