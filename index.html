Diagnostic Assistant

Mobile Network Troubleshooter

Select what you are experiencing to view root causes and technical recommendations.

Slow Internet Speed Diagnosis

Common Root Causes:
  • Tower Bandwidth Congestion: High density of active connections in the serving cell sector.
  • Low SINR (Noise Ratio): Severe interference from adjacent cell towers or structural shielding.
  • Fallback to 4G LTE: Connection dropped from high-frequency 5G NR band due to indoor wall attenuation.
NetScope Recommendation

Use NetScope to check real-time RSRP and SINR metrics. Moving closer to windows or cycling airplane mode helps latch onto cleaner frequency bands.
