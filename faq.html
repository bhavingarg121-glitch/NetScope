Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding cellular metrics, India network coverage, and Android device compatibility.

NetScope India is a mobile network intelligence platform consisting of an Android application for on-device hardware signal & speed measurements, and an interactive web portal for regional coverage maps and telecom intelligence.

The India Network Map visualizes regional 5G/4G coverage, speeds, and signal quality across Indian cities. Initially populated with calibrated baseline datasets, it is designed to ingest anonymized crowdsourced measurements from the NetScope Android community.

Yes, NetScope directly interfaces with the Android TelephonyManager framework and is compatible with all Indian cellular carriers across 5G SA, 5G NSA, 4G LTE, 3G, and 2G.

NetScope is 100% free, open-source, and contains zero advertisements or user tracking trackers.
